HIRO SWAP

A simple, non-custodial swap interface built for Robinhood Chain.

Documentation · Initial Release

Introduction

HIRO SWAP is a decentralized swap interface designed to make token swaps on Robinhood Chain simple and transparent.

HIRO SWAP does not custody user funds or private keys. Your wallet remains in control of every transaction and must approve and sign swaps.

How HIRO SWAP Works

HIRO SWAP provides the interface while on-chain smart contracts execute supported swaps. A typical swap follows four steps:

  1. Select the token you want to sell.
  2. Select the token you want to receive.
  3. Review the quote, route, gas, slippage and price impact.
  4. Confirm and sign the transaction in your wallet.

Connect Your Wallet

Connect a compatible EVM wallet and switch to Robinhood Chain when prompted. HIRO SWAP requires the wallet to authorize transactions; HIRO SWAP never asks for your seed phrase or private key.

Making a Swap

Choose From and To, enter the amount, review the available quote, then press Swap. Your wallet will show the transaction before it is signed.

Always verify the token, amount and transaction details in your wallet before signing.

Routing

HIRO SWAP uses on-chain liquidity infrastructure to source swap quotes. The interface can route supported swaps through available liquidity and presents the selected route before confirmation.

Routing and available liquidity can vary by token pair and pool availability.

Fees & Gas

Swap transactions require network gas paid in native ETH on Robinhood Chain. Additional liquidity or protocol fees may apply depending on the route and pool used.

Gas estimates shown in the interface are estimates and can change before confirmation.

Slippage & Price Impact

Slippage controls how much the received amount may move before a transaction is rejected. Price impact reflects the effect of your trade relative to available liquidity.

Higher slippage does not guarantee a better execution price. Use settings appropriate for the liquidity and volatility of the pair.

HIRO Points

Eligible confirmed swaps can earn HIRO Points.

1 Point / $0.10Base points per eligible swap volume
2× HIRO SwapHIRO Swap volume receives a 2× points multiplier

ETH → USDG is currently excluded from points.

Points are finalized only after the transaction is confirmed and verified by the backend.

Weekly Rewards

Weekly rewards are designed to be distributed proportionally according to eligible weekly HIRO Points.

User Reward = (User Weekly Points / Total Weekly Points) × Weekly ETH Reward Pool

Actual reward amounts depend on the funded weekly reward pool and finalized eligible points.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks wallets by weekly HIRO Points. Rankings are based on verified activity recorded by the protocol backend.

Security

HIRO SWAP is non-custodial. Wallet signatures are required for transactions, and users should independently verify transaction details before signing.

Risks & Limitations

  • Smart contracts and blockchain transactions carry technical and financial risk.
  • Token prices and liquidity can change rapidly.
  • Quotes, gas estimates and price impact are estimates until execution.
  • Transactions confirmed on-chain generally cannot be reversed by HIRO SWAP.

FAQ

Does HIRO SWAP custody my funds?

No. Your wallet remains in control of your assets and transaction signatures.

What network does HIRO SWAP use?

Robinhood Chain, Chain ID 4663.

When are HIRO Points finalized?

After an eligible swap is confirmed on-chain and verified by the backend.

Is every swap eligible for points?

No. Eligibility rules apply, including the current exclusion of ETH → USDG.
